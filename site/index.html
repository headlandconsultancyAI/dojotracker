How the filter works

Confirmed means the text uses clear redundancy language (redundancies, layoffs, made redundant, job cuts, consultation period, severance and similar, in English, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and French). Possible means only adjacent language, such as restructuring, reorgs or people being let go, so it's worth a human look. Phrases that only look like redundancy language, like "dismissive", "fired up" or "redundant meetings", are ignored.

LinkedIn posts also have to be about this company rather than another organisation with a similar name. A post counts if it mentions the company alongside payments language, a known person, or an author whose headline names the company. Posts about unrelated organisations are filtered out, and you can see them and the reason under Filtered out.

Tone on Glassdoor is the reviewer's star rating (1–2 negative, 3 mixed, 4–5 positive). On LinkedIn it's a word-based read of the post, so treat it as a guide. If something's in the wrong place, use Not relevant or Actually relevant on the card, then share the change with everyone using the banner that appears.
